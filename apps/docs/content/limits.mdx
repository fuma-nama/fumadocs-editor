## General

- **React 19 only.** Client component; SSR apps mount it inside
  `'use client'`. For a server-rendered first paint, pass your site's
  rendered MDX as `staticFallback`. The editor swaps in on hydration
  without a parse on the critical path.
- **Word pastes**: coerced through the schema (marks, tables, lists
  survive). One upstream exception: Word numbered lists arrive as literal
  `1. Item` paragraphs. That is ProseMirror's clipboard parser.
- **Deferred syntaxes**: `<CodeBlockTabs>`, primitive `<Tabs>`, and
  footnotes are verbatim-only for now ([details](/syntax#deferred-syntaxes)).

## Engine differences

Exercised on Chromium, WebKit, and Firefox (desktop and touch). Aligned,
except:

- **WebKit** takes one extra arrow-key stop when leaving inline math
  (ProseMirror boundary, cosmetic).
- Clicks on dead chrome (tab-strip padding, gap in a code-block header)
  are no-ops. Clicks on real component chrome place the caret in the
  nearest region.

## iOS

Touch is verified against real WebKit with touch events: the mobile bar
appears on coarse pointers, sticks to the bottom, 44 pt minimum targets.
Autocorrect, autocapitalize, and spellcheck are suppressed on the editing
surface.

Not yet verified on physical hardware:

- the bottom bar riding the on-screen keyboard (`visualViewport` inset)
- autocorrect and dictation (`insertReplacementText`, not key events)
- true iOS Safari rendering

## Collaboration

- A hard kill of the sync server can lose edits still inside its save
  debounce (under a second). Orderly shutdown flushes.
- Edits typed **while the server was down** are discarded when it comes
  back ([epoch reset](/collab#restarts-and-the-epoch-model)). Edits made
  while merely disconnected converge on reconnect.
- The raw MDX tab is disabled during collab.
