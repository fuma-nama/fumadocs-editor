## General

- **React 19 only.** The editor is a client component; server-rendered apps
  mount it inside a `'use client'` boundary. For a server-rendered first
  paint, pass your site's rendered MDX as `staticFallback` — the editor
  swaps in on hydration without a parse on the critical path.
- **Word pastes**: pasted content is coerced through the schema and cleans up
  well (marks, tables, lists survive), with one upstream exception —
  Word's numbered lists arrive as literal `1. Item` paragraphs. That is
  ProseMirror's clipboard parser, not a bug in your setup.
- **Deferred syntaxes**: `<CodeBlockTabs>`, primitive `<Tabs>`, and footnotes
  are verbatim-only for now ([details](/syntax#deferred-syntaxes)).

## Engine differences

The editor is exercised on Chromium, WebKit, and Firefox (desktop and touch
contexts). Behavior is aligned; what remains:

- **WebKit** takes one extra arrow-key stop when leaving inline math — a
  ProseMirror boundary artifact, cosmetic only.
- Clicks on dead chrome (tab-strip padding, the gap in a code-block header)
  are no-ops on every engine; clicks on real component chrome place the
  caret in the nearest region.

## iOS

Touch behavior is verified against real WebKit with touch events: the mobile
bar appears on coarse pointers, rides the bottom, and every target meets the
44 pt minimum; autocorrect, autocapitalize, and spellcheck are suppressed on
the editing surface.

Not yet verified on physical hardware, and worth checking in your own app:

- the bottom bar riding the on-screen keyboard (`visualViewport` inset),
- autocorrect and dictation, which insert text through
  `insertReplacementText` rather than key events,
- true iOS Safari rendering.

## Collaboration

- A hard kill of the sync server can lose edits still inside its save
  debounce — under a second. Orderly shutdown flushes.
- Edits typed **while the server was down** are discarded when it comes back
  (the [epoch reset](/collab#restarts-and-the-epoch-model)); edits made while
  merely disconnected converge on reconnect.
- The raw MDX tab is disabled during collaborative sessions.
