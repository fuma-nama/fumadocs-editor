---
title: Known Limits
icon: TriangleAlert
description: Platform caveats and current boundaries
---

- **React 19 only.** The editor is a client component. For a server-rendered
  first paint, pass your site's rendered MDX as `staticFallback`.
- **Word numbered lists** paste as plain `1. Item` paragraphs. Other
  formatting survives.
- **Not yet editable:** `<CodeBlockTabs>`, non-fumadocs `<Tabs>` and
  footnotes are kept [verbatim](/syntax#not-yet-editable).
- **Collaboration:** the MDX source tab is disabled, and edits made while the
  server is down are lost ([restarts](/collab#restarts)).
