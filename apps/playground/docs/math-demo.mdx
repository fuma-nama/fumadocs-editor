# Math

Inline math like $E = mc^2$ sits in a sentence, and display-style inline
$$c = \pm\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$$ keeps its double dollars.

$$
T_{f}(z)=\sum _{k=0}^{\infty }{\frac {(z-c)^{k}}{2\pi i}}\int _{\gamma }{\frac {f(w)}{(w-c)^{k+1}}}\,dw
$$

Prices like $5 stay editable as prose once escaped.
